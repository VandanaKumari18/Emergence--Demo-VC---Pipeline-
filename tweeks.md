### Product
Tweeks is a browser extension that allows users to modify any website in their browser to add functionality, filter or highlight content, re-theme, reorganize, or de-clutter. It uses natural language input, making it easier for non-technical users to customize websites without needing to write custom JavaScript or CSS. This is similar to userscript managers like Violentmonkey or Tampermonkey but with a more user-friendly approach. The product aims to simplify the process of customizing websites for individual users.

### Market
The market for browser extensions and website customization tools is sizable, with many competitors offering various solutions. However, the specific focus on natural language input for customization could differentiate Tweeks. The right time for this product could be now, given the increasing need for customization and personalization across the web. Competitors like Violentmonkey and Tampermonkey have established user bases, but Tweeks' user-friendly approach might attract a broader audience.
